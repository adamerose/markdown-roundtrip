# Hard Line Breaks

## Trailing Two Spaces

Line with two trailing spaces  
followed by continuation.

Three trailing spaces   
also a hard break.

## Trailing Backslash

Line with backslash\
followed by continuation.

## Mixed in Same Paragraph

First hard break (spaces)  
second hard break (backslash)\
third line continues.

## Soft Break (No Trailing Syntax)

Normal line
with soft break.

## In Blockquotes

> Hard break with spaces  
> inside a blockquote.

> Hard break with backslash\
> inside a blockquote.

## In List Items

- List item with hard break  
  continues here.
- Another item with backslash\
  continues here.

## Consecutive Hard Breaks

Line one  
line two  
line three.
