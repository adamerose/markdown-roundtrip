# Emoji Shortcodes

Standalone: :smile: :rocket: :warning: :thumbsup:

Inline with text: Hello :wave: world!

Inside formatting: **:star: bold** and *:heart: italic*.

Adjacent to punctuation: :smile:, :rocket:. :warning:!

Non-shortcode colons: time is 10:30 AM, visit http://example.com.

Multiple: :one: :two: :three: in a row.

## In Block Contexts

- List with :emoji: shortcode
- Another :rocket: item
